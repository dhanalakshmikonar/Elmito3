What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, mobile number, username.
  • Delivery details: shipping address and phone number.
  • Order history and payment status. Card and UPI details are handled by Razorpay and are never stored by us.
  • Messages you send us, and your email if you subscribe to our newsletter.

How we use it

  • To create your account, verify it, and process and deliver your orders.
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates by email and WhatsApp.
  • To prevent fraud and keep your account secure.
  • To send newsletters, only if you've subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run the store: our payment processor (Razorpay), messaging providers for email and WhatsApp, and delivery partners. We do not sell your data.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and to protect forms from forgery. We don't use advertising cookies.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by contacting us.

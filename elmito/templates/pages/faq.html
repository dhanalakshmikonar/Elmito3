How do I find my size?

Use the size guide on any product page. Measurements are for the garment, laid flat. If you're between sizes, size up for a relaxed fit.

How long does delivery take?

Orders dispatch within 2 working days and usually arrive in 3–7 days across India. You'll get an email and a WhatsApp message when your order ships.

How much is shipping?

Free on orders above {{ FREE_SHIPPING_THRESHOLD|inr }}. Below that, a flat {{ FLAT_SHIPPING_CHARGE|inr }}.

What payment methods do you accept?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking and wallets, processed securely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card details.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes. Unworn items with tags can be returned within 7 days of delivery. See the returns policy.

Why do I need to verify with a code?

We send a one-time code to confirm your email when you sign up, and to confirm orders before payment. It protects your account and prevents fraudulent orders.

How do I wash my T-shirt?

Machine wash cold with similar colours, then tumble dry low or hang dry. Avoid bleach.

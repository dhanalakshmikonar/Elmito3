Our point of view

Good things

EL MITO means “the myth” in Spanish. We chose it as a reminder of the idea we're pushing against: that a good T-shirt is a throwaway.

01The everyday, rethought

The most-worn thing in your wardrobe deserves more thought.

It's the garment people wear most and think about least. A T-shirt sits against your skin all day, goes through the wash hundreds of times, and ends up in every outfit. It deserves the same care as a jacket.

Returns

You can return an item within 7 days of delivery if it is unworn, unwashed, and has its original tags and packaging.

How to start a return

  • Contact us with your order number and the item you'd like to return.
  • We'll confirm eligibility and arrange a pickup or give you a return address.

Exchanges

We can exchange for another size or colour, subject to availability.

Refunds

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days of us receiving and inspecting the item. Original shipping charges are not refundable unless the item was faulty or incorrect.

Faulty or incorrect items

If you receive something damaged or not what you ordered, tell us within 48 hours of delivery with photos and we'll replace or refund it at no cost to you.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled before they ship. After that, the returns process applies.
